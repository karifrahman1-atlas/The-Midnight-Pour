THE CRAFT

Cold brew vs iced coffee: choose by flavour

Iced coffee is typically brewed hot and chilled, so it can taste brighter and more aromatic. Cold brew steeps in cool water for longer and often tastes rounder and less sharp. Neither is automatically stronger; ask whether the café serves cold brew as a concentrate or ready to drink.

COFFEE BASICS · 4 MIN READ

Espresso vs. Americano: what's the difference?

An espresso is a concentrated shot. An Americano adds hot water, creating a longer, lighter-bodied drink while keeping the espresso flavour.

COLD COFFEE · 3 MIN READ

Cold brew and iced coffee aren't the same

Iced coffee is usually brewed hot and cooled over ice. Cold brew steeps coffee in cool water over a longer period, often giving it a rounder taste.

PLANNING A COFFEE STOP?

Good to know
before you come.

Menu, atmosphere, location and the details that make planning a meet-up easier.

I prefer coffee that is not too bitter. What should I try?+

Start by deciding whether you want milk, sweetness or a black coffee. A flat white is milk-smooth but still coffee-forward; a mocha adds chocolate; an iced latte is chilled and creamy. If you prefer black coffee, compare an Americano with cold brew. Ask the team about the current recipe and sweetness level.

Can I bring my laptop and work?+

Seating and facilities can vary throughout the day. Contact the café before visiting specifically to work or study, especially if you need Wi-Fi or a power outlet.

Can I work or study there for a few hours?+

That depends on the day’s crowd, seating policy, Wi-Fi and available sockets. Contact the café first if you need to work for an extended period, rather than assuming every table is a workstation.

Can I visit on my own?+

Absolutely. A coffee break can be a solo ritual, a quick reset between errands, or time to catch up with someone. Seating availability may vary during busy hours.

Can I see the menu before I arrive?+

Yes, browse the coffee categories on this page to compare drinks before you visit. Prices, recipes and availability should be confirmed with the café before ordering.

Where are you located?+

The café is listed in Dhanmondi, Dhaka. Check the area and plan your route on Maps; confirm the exact address before travelling.

What should I confirm before travelling from another part of Dhaka?+

Confirm the exact map pin, opening hours and current menu. If you are coming as a group, ask about seating availability before everyone sets off.

What are your opening hours?+

Opening hours can change on holidays or special occasions. Check the latest hours before setting out.
